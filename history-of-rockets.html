History of Rockets

A poster series translating three moments from rocket history into bold illustration—each with a motion study, plus large-format static prints for selected episodes and a series key image for the wall.

Poster Design Motion Illustration 2019

Project brief

From chronicle to poster

Rocket history is dense with dates and engineering detail. The design goal was to distill three distinct stories into posters that read in seconds yet reward a longer look—using strong silhouettes, directional composition, and a controlled palette so each episode feels related but not repetitive.

Most episodes pair a motion poster (trajectory, timing, energy) with a static print for texture, line work, and typography at full resolution; the finale is motion-led, with the series hero image tying the set together on the wall.

Role
Concept, illustration, motion, layout
Deliverables
3× motion posters · 2× static prints · series hero
Tools
Adobe CC (video / animation pipeline), print finishing
Format
Portrait motion · landscape & portrait print

Design intent

  • Visual hierarchy

    Large focal forms and a single clear read path per poster so the story lands before the fine detail.

  • Motion vs. print

    Motion emphasizes sequence and thrust; print preserves illustration craft and typographic lockups for close viewing.

  • Series cohesion

    Shared grid logic and recurring graphic motifs tie the three episodes into one exhibition set.
