History of Rockets

Three motion posters created in Cinema 4D, each telling a pivotal story from the history of rocketry. 3D scenes combine dramatic lighting, camera animation, and typographic overlays to bring these historical moments to life.

Cinema 4D Motion Poster 3D 2019

Overview

Three stories, three posters

This project explores the visual storytelling of rocket history through three motion posters, each based on a real historical episode. All scenes were modeled, lit, and animated in Cinema 4D, then composited with typographic elements to produce dynamic poster-format narratives.

Each episode uses a distinct visual language—materials, camera movement, and color—to match the tone of its story, while sharing a consistent format so the three work together as a cohesive series.

Role
3D modeling, lighting, animation, compositing
Deliverables
3 motion posters + static key frames
Tools
Cinema 4D, After Effects
Year
2019
Episode 01

The Origin of Rockets

The first story traces the earliest propulsion experiments—from ancient steam-powered devices to the first true rockets. The C4D scene uses warm, earthy tones and slow upward camera movement to evoke the dawn of flight.

Episode 02

The Space Race

The second story captures the intensity of the Cold War space race. Metallic materials, dramatic side-lighting, and faster camera pacing in C4D convey the urgency and industrial scale of the era.

Episode 03

Into Deep Space

The final story looks outward—past Earth orbit and into deep space exploration. The C4D scene shifts to cooler tones, wider lenses, and slower orbital camera paths to evoke the vastness and quiet ambition of interplanetary travel.
