Coolita OS

Coolita OS, as a lightweight Web OS, based on minimal interaction and convenient and smooth operation experience, put forward the design concept of "Cool & Clear" at the beginning of its design. We aim to create a trendy, cool and concise smart TV system for users, providing you with a lighter, smoother and more convenient smart TV experience.
